## Why
The maintainer: "you can push to main as long as there is no reason to uprev firmware or python versions".

## What changed
- Commits that bump no version (nothing under `firmware/` or `python/`: `.claude/`, `CLAUDE.md`, README,
  skills) may be committed on `main` and pushed to `origin/main` without asking.
- Commits that bump the firmware or Python patch still go on a branch off `main`, and are only pushed or merged
  when asked.
